Ring: this frame's label (green visible, orange hard to see). Faint dots: your labels on the two previous frames. The magnifier shows the area round the cursor 8 times enlarged; the click lands where its crosshair meets.

A frame takes a label only once it has been on screen for a quarter of a second (again after you come back to the window, so the click that brings it back saves nothing), and a held key labels one frame only. A click or key that comes too soon, or a click in which the pointer moved, saves nothing and says so in red.
